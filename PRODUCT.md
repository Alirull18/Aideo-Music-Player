# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Aideo serves desktop listeners who care about playback fidelity, local library control, and music discovery. Its primary users include audiophiles with high-resolution collections and external DACs, as well as listeners exploring unusual, international, or community-sourced music.

## Product Purpose

Aideo combines a native, high-fidelity audio engine with a flexible desktop music library, cloud and streaming connections, synchronized lyrics, and discovery tools. Success means listeners can move from selecting a track to understanding and enjoying its sound with little friction, while retaining clear control over their library, output device, and playback path.

## Brand Personality

Technical, immersive, personal. The interface should make audio quality legible without becoming clinical, use motion to make playback feel present without interrupting control, and let artwork and lyrics carry the emotional character of the current track.

## Anti-references

Avoid dated desktop players that bury audio routing and metadata in dense utility dialogs. Avoid closed streaming surfaces that treat local files as secondary. Avoid generic dashboard card grids, decorative visualizer clutter, and motion that competes with the music or obscures standard controls.

## Design Principles

- Make the audio path understandable at a glance.
- Let the current track shape the atmosphere without reducing readability.
- Use motion as playback feedback and tactile response, not as decoration.
- Keep familiar controls predictable and keyboard accessible.
- Prefer progressive detail so the main listening view stays calm.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard shortcuts and focus states are first-class controls. Motion must provide a reduced-motion alternative, preserve readable contrast, and never be required to understand or operate the player. No additional assistive technology requirements are documented.
