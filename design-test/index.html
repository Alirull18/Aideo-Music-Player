The rack

Each feature ships as its own module. Turn on what you need; everything runs locally on your machine.

EQ-01

AutoEQ calibration

Search your exact headphone or IEM model, pick a target curve, and Aideo writes the corrective parametric filters for you. Four thousand models covered, gain headroom protected automatically.

DSP-02

Parametric equalizer

A 5-band quick board or a full 10-band parametric, computed in real time with 64-bit biquad precision. Export your curve as an Equalizer APO preset when you want it elsewhere.

LYR-03

Synced lyrics with Romaji

Timed lyrics scroll line by line while the track plays. One click transliterates Japanese to Romaji, pulled from LRCLIB, NetEase, and QQ Music.

LIB-04

Hybrid library

Local FLAC and DSD sit in the same playlists as YouTube Music streams, Tidal Hi-Fi tracks, and Subsonic or Jellyfin servers. One queue, every source.

CON-05

Aideo Connect remote

Scan a QR code and control playback from any phone browser on your Wi-Fi. No companion app, no account, no traffic leaving your network.

WRP-06

Private listening stats

A Wrapped-style year in review, generated on your disk from your own play history. The data never leaves the machine because it never exists anywhere else.

Questions from the bench

Does bit-perfect output actually matter if I can't hear it?

Maybe not, and that's fine. But resampling and limiter compression are measurable on any DAC, and once you've paid for good transducers it costs nothing to stop mangling the signal before it reaches them.

Which headphones work with AutoEQ?

Over four thousand models are in the database: Sennheiser, Beyerdynamic, Sony, Audio-Technica, most popular IEMs, and AirPods. Search by name, apply the curve, tweak it if you disagree with Harman.

Is streaming quality worse than local files?

Tidal Hi-Fi tracks are lossless and play back bit-perfect like local files. YouTube Music streams are lossy, so they're transcoded once on arrival and then handled by the same engine; Aideo marks them honestly in the library instead of pretending otherwise.

What does it cost?

Nothing. Aideo is free and open source under its repository license, and there is no account, telemetry, or subscription anywhere in the app.

How is this different from foobar2000 or MusicBee?

Those are excellent local players. Aideo starts from the same bit-perfect principle and adds what they treat as plugins or don't attempt: built-in AutoEQ without configuration, Romaji lyric transliteration, hybrid streaming sources, and a phone remote over LAN.
