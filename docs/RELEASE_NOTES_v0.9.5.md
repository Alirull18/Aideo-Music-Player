# 💎 Aideo Music Player v0.9.5 — The Discovery & Performance Overhaul

Welcome to **Aideo v0.9.5**! This release brings a massive intelligence upgrade to our **Personalized Discovery Hub**, full artist discography browsing, strict anti-collision candidate verification, slop elimination, plus comprehensive engine performance optimizations with zero idle resource usage.

---

## 🌟 Highlights & Key Features

### 🧠 Smarter AI Discovery Hub
* **Multi-Tier Recommendation Pipeline**: Blends collaborative similar tracks, related artist neighborhoods, deep catalog cuts, micro-genre waves, and ListenBrainz embeddings for rich, authentic music discoveries.
* **Smart Anti-Collision Verification**: Completely prevents unrelated songs with the same title from infiltrating your recommendations.
* **Expanded Discovery Pool**: Delivers **40–75+ verified recommendations** per fetch with interactive *"Show More"* pagination and 1-click *"Discover More Fresh Tracks"*.

### 🎵 Complete Artist Discography & Live Search Filter
* **3-Tab Discography Layout**: Switch instantly between **🔥 Popular Hits**, **🎵 All Releases & Singles**, and **📁 In Library**.
* **Studio Discography Engine**: Fetches official studio albums, singles, and b-sides directly from official YouTube Music topic channels.
* **Live In-Profile Song Search**: Instantly find any b-side or single directly within the artist's profile.

### 🚫 Zero-Tolerance Slop & Reaction Elimination
* **Pure Music Search**: Search and discovery engines automatically detect and strip out reaction videos, vocal coach reviews, fancams, vlog clips, karaoke covers, and tutorials.

### ⚡ 0% Idle CPU & Battery Optimization
* **Whisper-Quiet Efficiency**: Canvas visualizers, frequency waterfalls, liquid art shaders, and 60 FPS karaoke timers automatically sleep when audio is paused or the window is minimized.
* **Shallow State Architecture**: Prevents unnecessary background re-rendering on playback ticks.

### 📌 Pinned Mini Player & Multi-Monitor Fullscreen
* **Always-on-Top Pin**: Keep the compact Mini Player floating over your code editor, browser, or full-screen games.
* **True Multi-Screen Borderless Fullscreen**: Enter immersive fullscreen on your active monitor without mouse lock or minimize issues when clicking on second screens.

### 🎨 Adaptive Album Artwork & Bulk Library Actions
* **Non-Square Cover Art Fit**: Rectangular singles and Japanese Obi covers display without cropping, backed by a dynamic color-matched ambient blur aura.
* **Multi-Select Action Bar**: Select multiple songs with `Ctrl + Click`, `Shift + Click`, or `Ctrl + A` to batch play, queue, favorite, or add to playlists.

### 🔄 Resilient Streaming & Session Memory
* **Automatic Network Reconnection**: Absorbs Wi-Fi hiccups and resumes playback with HTTP byte-range resume without restarting the track.
* **Complete Session Persistence**: Remembers volume, view modes, album sort preferences, and window geometry across restarts.
* **System Tray Minimization**: Keeps audio playing in the background when clicking the window close button.
