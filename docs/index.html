Why Direct Hardware Transport Matters

Standard players pass your music through the Windows Audio Engine mixer, which forcibly resamples and compresses audio. Aideo locks your DAC hardware directly for pure bit-perfect playback.

Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers to common questions about bit-perfect playback, AutoEQ calibrations, and system integration.

Aideo Music Player is an open-source, high-fidelity desktop audio engine designed for audiophiles, headphone enthusiasts, and music collectors on Windows 10 & 11. It combines bit-perfect WASAPI hardware locking with modern fluid UI, 4,000+ AutoEQ headphone calibrations, and synchronized Romaji lyrics.
Standard Windows media players send audio through the Windows Audio Engine (Shared Mode), which resamples all streams to a fixed rate (typically 48kHz) and introduces limiter compression. Aideo's WASAPI Exclusive mode locks your DAC clock to the exact native resolution of the audio file (up to 384kHz / 32-bit float), ensuring bit-perfect fidelity with zero resampler distortion.
Aideo integrates Jaakko Pasanen's renowned AutoEQ database containing over 4,000 calibrated target curves. Simply search for your headphone or IEM model, and Aideo will instantly calculate and load high-precision biquad parametric filters with automatic negative preamp gain to prevent clipping.
Yes! Aideo features a unified hybrid library engine. You can mix your local DSD/FLAC/WAV audio collection with YouTube Music streams, Tidal Hi-Fi tracks, and Subsonic/Jellyfin cloud servers inside unified playlists with synchronized metadata and lyrics.
Aideo includes a built-in lightweight local network server. When enabled in Settings, any phone or tablet on the same Wi-Fi network can visit the local IP or scan the QR code to instantly control playback, adjust volume, view real-time lyrics, and browse queues without installing any third-party app.
Click the Cast icon in the playback bar. Aideo uses SSDP discovery to instantly find DLNA / UPnP compatible smart TVs, AV receivers, and Wi-Fi speakers on your local network. Audio is streamed in high-fidelity PCM format directly to your audio hardware.
Yes! Right-click any track in your library and choose “Edit Track Info”. You can edit title, artist, album, genre, track number, and drag-and-drop album cover art. Aideo writes ID3, FLAC Vorbis, and MP4 tags losslessly via its built-in Rust Lofty engine.
Desktop Lyrics HUD creates a transparent, floating lyric bar on top of all Windows applications. Pressing Alt + L locks the HUD in click-through mode, allowing you to click games and work windows underneath without interruption.
Aideo includes 5 bespoke visualizer modes adhering to its Dark Obsidian Studio aesthetic: Studio Peak-Decay Bars (with gravity-governed falling peak caps), Bilateral Mirror Spectrum, Analog Oscilloscope Silk Ribbon, Radial Halo Orbit, and Phosphor LED Dot-Matrix. The engine renders on hardware-accelerated HTML5 Canvas with High-DPI auto-scaling. When playback is paused or stopped, audio decays exponentially into an ambient breathing resting state, automatically cutting CPU/GPU usage to 0%. Users can also select 30/60/120 FPS caps or low-spec mode in Settings.
Aideo eliminates audible gap clicks and micro-pauses by parsing audio container metadata (such as iTunSMPB) and truncating encoder delay and padding frames with sample accuracy. Paired with monotonic clock smoothing, continuous live albums and symphony movements stream seamlessly. In True Bit-Perfect Mode, all DSP modules (EQ, crossfeed, pitch, limiter) are fully bypassed with unity 1.0 digital volume and dither suppression for uncolored bit-for-bit delivery.
