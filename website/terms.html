3. Audio Hardware & Audiophile Safety Warning

Aideo provides advanced low-level audio hardware features, specifically WASAPI Exclusive Mode and Bit-Perfect Direct Hardware Streaming. In these modes:

  • The software communicates directly with your external digital-to-analog converter (DAC) or audio interface, bypassing the operating system's software volume mixer and limiter.
  • In true bit-perfect mode, the audio stream is transmitted with unity gain (full digital scale 0 dBFS) directly to your DAC.
  • User Precaution: Always check your physical amplifier or headphone amplifier volume knobs before initiating playback in Exclusive Mode to prevent hearing damage or equipment overdrive. The contributors to Aideo Music Player accept no liability for any equipment damage or auditory discomfort resulting from extreme volume settings.
