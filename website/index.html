Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about setting up and using Aideo Music Player.

What is WASAPI Exclusive mode and why is it superior to standard playback?

Standard Windows music players send audio to the Windows Shared Audio Engine. The operating system forces all streams into a common sample rate (usually 48kHz), applies software dithering, and routes audio through the CAudioLimiter compressor to prevent system sounds from clipping.

Aideo locks your DAC in WASAPI Exclusive mode, completely bypassing the Windows mixer. The exact bits contained in your high-resolution files are transferred directly to the audio hardware, maintaining bit-perfect fidelity, uncompressed dynamic range, and accurate stereo imaging.

What is AutoEQ and how does it improve my headphones?

AutoEQ is an open-source database of precision acoustic measurements for over 4,000 headphone and in-ear monitor models. Every headphone has physical acoustic peaks and resonances that color sound.

Aideo integrates AutoEQ natively. You simply select your headphone model, and Aideo automatically applies compensatory parametric biquad equalizer filters to tune your headphones to the acoustic Harman target curve, restoring natural timbre and balanced bass.

How does Aideo compare to foobar2000 and MusicBee?

While foobar2000 and MusicBee are legendary tools, configuring bit-perfect output, modern visualizers, and lyrics often requires searching for third-party DLL plugins and navigating complex configuration dialogs.

Aideo delivers audiophile sound out of the box with modern craftsmanship: a fluid liquid-glass interface, 5 immersive Theater Mode archetypes, synchronized Romaji karaoke lyrics, native AutoEQ without plugins, and Aideo Connect for wireless smartphone control.

How do I control playback from my smartphone using Aideo Connect?

Aideo includes a built-in LAN web remote server. When enabled in Settings, Aideo generates a local QR code and URL on your home Wi-Fi network. Scanning the QR code opens a lightweight responsive web remote on your smartphone with instant access to track playback, volume, and queue management without installing any phone app.

Is Aideo really 100% free and open source?

Yes. Aideo is released under the permissive MIT license. There are no subscriptions, no ads, no feature paywalls, and no telemetry tracking. The full source code is publicly inspectable and auditable on GitHub.
