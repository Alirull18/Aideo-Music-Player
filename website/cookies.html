Cookie & Local Storage Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026. A clear explanation of what we store locally and why we reject advertising trackers.

1. What Are Cookies and Local Storage?

Cookies are small text files placed on your computer or mobile device when you browse websites. Modern web standards also provide Web Storage (`localStorage` and `sessionStorage`), which allow web pages to store small amounts of state data directly in your browser without transmitting them back and forth with every server request.

2. Our Zero-Tracking Philosophy

Most modern commercial websites use cookies to track your behavior across the internet, construct marketing profiles, and serve targeted advertisements.

Aideo rejects this paradigm entirely. We do not deploy third-party advertising cookies, fingerprinting scripts, tracking pixels, or cross-site tracking beacons.

3. Detailed Inventory of Client-Side Storage

Below is a complete, transparent breakdown of the keys stored by this website in your browser's `localStorage`:

Storage Key Type Duration Purpose
aideo_cookie_consent_v1 Strictly Necessary Persistent Remembers your choice on the cookie consent banner so it does not reappear on every page visit.
aideo_theme_pref Functional Persistent Remembers your visual console theme preference (Obsidian dark studio).
aideo_vis_mode Functional Persistent Stores your active mode in the interactive audio visualizer preview.

4. Classification of Storage Categories

4.1 Strictly Necessary Storage

These items are essential for the website to function correctly and cannot be switched off in our systems. They are typically set in response to actions made by you, such as setting your privacy preferences or navigating between pages.

4.2 Functional Preferences

These items enable the website to provide enhanced personalization and remember selections (such as the interactive visualizer mode you last explored). If you disable these, the site will revert to default settings each time you reload.

4.3 Performance & Analytics (Disabled)

We do not use Google Analytics, Cloudflare Web Analytics, or any analytics trackers on this website. This category is permanently disabled in our software.

4.4 Targeting & Advertising (Disabled)

We do not host advertisements, partner with advertising networks, or allow third parties to monitor your browsing behavior. This category is permanently disabled.

5. How to Manage or Clear Your Storage

You can change your preferences at any time using our on-page settings dialog or through your web browser controls:

Alternatively, you can delete all cookies and local storage through your browser settings:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Delete browsing data → Cookies and other site data.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data → Clear Data.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies and site data.
  • Apple Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data.

6. Updates to This Policy

We may update our Cookie Policy periodically to reflect changes in legal requirements or software updates. The updated version will always be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
