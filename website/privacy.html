COMPLIANCE & TRANSPARENCY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026. Effective immediately for all versions of Aideo Music Player and this website.

1. Executive Summary & Core Principle

Aideo Music Player ("Aideo", "we", "us", or "our") is designed from the ground up as an offline-first, zero-telemetry desktop application. We believe your musical tastes, listening history, and local audio files are private personal property.

Unlike conventional streaming platforms and proprietary media players, we do not collect, track, store, or monetize your listening habits, search queries, IP addresses, or personal audio collection contents.

2. Data Collection Practices: Desktop Application

2.1 Local Audio Files and Metadata

When you point Aideo to your local music folders, the embedded SQLite database creates a local full-text search index (FTS5) solely on your computer's local storage. This indexed metadata (artist names, album titles, file paths, sample rates) never leaves your local machine. No telemetry or inventory data is ever transmitted to us or any remote server.

2.2 Zero Telemetry and Diagnostics

Aideo does not contain Google Analytics, Sentry, Mixpanel, Crashlytics, or any other telemetry framework. Application crash logs and playback statistics are stored strictly on your local disk in your user data directory and are never uploaded automatically.

2.3 Optional Network Services Initiated by You

When you explicitly use features requiring network connectivity, direct connections are established strictly to fulfill your request:

  • Synchronized Lyrics (LRCLIB, Musixmatch): When a song plays and local lyrics are missing, Aideo queries public lyric databases using the track title and artist name to fetch synchronized lyrics. No user identifiers or IP addresses are logged by Aideo.
  • Aideo Connect (LAN Remote): The mobile remote control server runs exclusively on your local home network (LAN). It does not route through any cloud relay, STUN server, or external signaling server.
  • Auto-Updater: When checking for updates, Aideo queries the official GitHub Releases API (`api.github.com`). This request is governed by GitHub's Privacy Policy.

3. Data Collection Practices: Website

This website (`alirull18.github.io/Aideo-Music-Player`) is hosted as static open-source documentation. We do not use third-party advertising cookies, cross-site trackers, or user behavioral profiling scripts.

We use HTML5 `localStorage` strictly to preserve your selected UI preferences (such as dark mode preferences, audio visualizer simulator state, and your cookie consent preference). You can review our full details in the Cookie Policy.

4. European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)

Under the GDPR (Regulation EU 2016/679), individuals located in the European Economic Area (EEA) have specific legal rights regarding their personal data:

  • Right of Access and Portability: Because all your listening data and playlists reside exclusively on your local computer in an open SQLite database, you already possess complete, unfettered access to all your data at all times.
  • Right to Rectification and Erasure: You can edit audio tags or delete playlists and history directly inside the application, which immediately modifies or deletes the local database records without any retention by us.
  • Legal Basis for Processing: We do not process personal data on remote servers. Any ephemeral network request initiated by you (such as fetching lyrics) is processed based on your direct instruction (Contractual Necessity / Consent).

5. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act of 2018 (CCPA) and the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA):

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not share your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • We do not collect sensitive personal information as defined by California law.

6. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA)

Aideo Music Player does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. Because the application operates offline without requiring user registration, no age verification is requested.

7. Open Source Code Verification

Because Aideo is open-source software licensed under GPLv3, our source code is publicly inspectable by any security researcher or user. You can verify every network socket, IPC call, and database operation directly in our public repository at GitHub.
